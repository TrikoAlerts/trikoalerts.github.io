How to read the table:
  • Cut / Hold / Hike – the probability that the policy rate after the given meeting will be lower / unchanged / higher than today (cumulative, measured from today's level).
  • Change (bp) – expected change in the rate at this single meeting. 25 bp = 0.25 percentage points.
  • Cumulative (bp) – sum of expected changes from today up to and including this meeting.
  • * – provisional date, the central bank may still change it.
Frequently asked questions
What are central bank rate probabilities?

They are market-implied estimates of how likely a central bank is to cut, hold or hike its policy rate at each upcoming meeting. They are derived from the OIS (Overnight Index Swap) market and updated automatically.

What do cut, hold and hike mean?

Cut means the policy rate is lowered, hold means it stays at the same level, and hike means it is raised. The percentages in the table show the probability of each outcome after the given meeting, measured from today's rate.

What are basis points (bp)?

A basis point is one hundredth of a percentage point. A change of 25 bp equals 0.25 percentage points.

How often is the data updated?

The page refreshes its data every 30 seconds while it is open in a browser. The valuation time and the last update time are shown at the top of the page.

Which central banks are covered?

The Federal Reserve (USD), European Central Bank (EUR), Bank of England (GBP), Bank of Japan (JPY), Bank of Canada (CAD), Reserve Bank of Australia (AUD), Swiss National Bank (CHF) and Reserve Bank of New Zealand (NZD).
