How to read the table:
  • Cut / Hold / Hike – the probability that the policy rate after the given meeting will be lower / unchanged / higher than today (cumulative, measured from today's level).
  • Change (bp) – expected change in the rate at this single meeting. 25 bp = 0.25 percentage points.
  • Cumulative (bp) – sum of expected changes from today up to and including this meeting.
  • * – provisional date, the central bank may still change it.
