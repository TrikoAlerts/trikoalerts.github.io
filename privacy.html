Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

This website, Interest Rate Probability (trikoalerts.github.io), is operated by Triko Alerts. It shows market-implied probabilities of central bank rate decisions. You can contact us at trikoalerts@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or to enter any personal information. The site has no forms, comments or user profiles.

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure traffic. It may collect:

  • pages visited and time spent on them,
  • approximate location (country, region or city), derived from your network address,
  • device type, operating system, browser and screen size,
  • how you arrived at the site (for example, a search engine or a link).

This data is used only in aggregate to understand how many people visit the site and to improve it. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Cookies and local storage

  • Analytics cookies (set by Google Analytics, e.g. _ga and _ga_*): used to tell visitors and sessions apart. They are set only after you click “Accept”.
  • Your choice: your decision (accepted or declined) is saved in your browser’s local storage under the name cookie_consent, so that we do not ask you again on every page. It is not sent to any server.

Until you accept, analytics storage is denied. Advertising-related storage is always denied.

4. Legal basis

We process analytics data only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent is voluntary, and you can use the whole site if you decline.

5. Google as a service provider

Analytics data is processed by Google, which may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. You can read how Google handles data in its own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention of analytics data is set in our Google Analytics account and is limited to the period we configure there.

6. Changing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your decision at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of the main pages. It clears your saved choice and shows the cookie notice again. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because analytics data is not linked to your name or account, we may not be able to identify data that relates to you. To ask a question or exercise a right, write to trikoalerts@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
